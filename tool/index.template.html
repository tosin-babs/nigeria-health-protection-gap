Would the pool survive?

Members pay what they can afford; the state subsidy makes up the rest. Too little subsidy and the scheme runs out of money — the chance of that is its probability of ruin.

How the solvency number is worked out

The pool's surplus each year is Uₜ = Uₜ₋₁ + n(C + s)(1 − e) − Sₜ: opening funds, plus contributions C and subsidy s from every one of n members net of an expense ratio e, less the year's claims Sₜ. Ruin means the surplus goes below zero in any year of the horizon.

Claims are drawn from the cost distribution of informal-sector individuals in the survey. The paper draws each pool-year as a multinomial over the 750 distinct cost values; that is too slow for a browser, so this page matches the first three moments of the same distribution with a translated gamma and draws 4,000 paths. Checked against the paper's own simulation, the two agree to within 0.35% at the 99.9th percentile of annual claims for every pool size and take-up pattern shown here. The minimum subsidy is found by bisection on a fixed set of draws, so moving a lever never changes the answer through simulation noise alone.

What this is, and what it is not

Read this before quoting a number

This calculator accompanies a working paper that measures Nigeria's health-protection gap and prices insurance for the informal sector. It runs the paper's own model on the paper's own data, in your browser. Set the levers back to the paper's settings and you get the paper's published figures.

The benefit package is defined by the parameters on the left, not by the NHIA's published tariff schedule, which is not available in machine-readable form. The share of spending inside the package, the drug co-payment and the demand response are assumptions — that is precisely why they are levers rather than constants. Costs come from a four-week recall window scaled to a year, the single largest source of uncertainty in the underlying estimates.

Figures are per person per year in naira. Nothing here is advice on which premium a scheme should charge.
